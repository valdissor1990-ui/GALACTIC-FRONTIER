# GALACTIC FRONTIER — Narrative Bible

Status: design locked for production planning. Not yet implemented in gameplay systems.
Language of in-game text target: Russian (source design may stay bilingual in docs).

**Tone:** dry frontier realism, rare warmth, no “chosen one saves the galaxy”.
**Theme:** whoever owns the map owns other people’s lives.
**Player company:** Orion Pathfinders (Орион-Следопыты).

This document must not copy names, characters, plot, or protected elements from Freelancer. Genre tropes only.

---

## 1. World

### Two galaxies

- **Ysera (Исера)** — home galaxy: stations, debt, corporations, lived-in border life.
- **Korvax (Корвакс)** — outer arm: fewer docks, stranger beacons, different docking law, more empty systems.

### Starting region: The Wedge Belt (Пояс Клина)

A chain of systems on the edge of reliable charts. Weak local authority. Repair docks, grey captains, small houses, and agents of larger powers.

### The ancient network: The Thread (Нить)

Not magic. Old navigation infrastructure: nodes, gates, archive beacons. Control of the Thread means control of routes, tariffs, and the ability to choke whole sectors.

### Core conflict

Three powers want the Thread:

| Faction | Goal |
|---------|------|
| Consortium **Vector** (Консорциум «Вектор») | Monopoly and profit |
| **Ring Guard** (Стражи Кольца) | Control framed as safety |
| Cult **Star Ash** (Пепел Звёзд) | Open everything; hear the “voice” of the network |

The player begins only wanting to clear company debt, then becomes a key-holder.

---

## 2. Player company — Orion Pathfinders

Independent contract outfit: recon, escort, search-and-retrieve. Not a navy, not a pirate banner.

**Start state:**

- one working ship;
- debt to shipyard **Dry Dock 9** (Сухой Док 9);
- archive of old routes from missing founder **Arcadis Voln** (Аркадий Вольн);
- reputation: “still breathing”.

**Company growth (meta progression):**

1. Clear the debt.
2. Hire agent / mechanic / fixer.
3. Hangar slot.
4. Branch office on a second station.
5. Specialization: Recon / Escort / Grey Contract.
6. House-level contracts (not only personal license jobs).

## 3. Key characters

### Arcadis Voln — founder (missing)
Voice in logs. Pragmatist with guilt. Left the company and the problem.

> “If you are reading this, I am dead or past the charts that lie. Do not sell the Thread to the first smile. A smile on the frontier is an invoice.”

### Mara Kell — Dry Dock 9 manager
Hard, honest about debt, not a cartoon villain. Pressure exists because the dock dies without it.

**Speech:** short, practical, sometimes tired-sharp.

### Yun Tarek — Vector liaison
Polite. Always “mutually beneficial”. Betrays softly.

### Commander Seva Rinn — Ring Guard
Discipline, regulation, contempt for grey captains. Respects kept word.

### Ilsa “Ash” — Star Ash voice
Speaks in riddles with fanatic logic underneath. May save the player once — and bill it in belief.

### Nex — informant on station Anchor-4 (Якорь-4)
Sells rumors. Likes living. Fears big players.

### “Cap” Orsa — free captain, rival
Mirror of the player. Ally or race for the same contract.

### Boatswain Rhea — optional companion / agent
Ex-Ring technician. Left. Quiet about the past. Best lines in repair bays and before fights.

## 4. Main plot structure

### Prologue — License and Clamp (30–40 min)

Hangar of Dock 9. Ship almost ready. Mara holds a debt chip.

**Mara:** Sign here. And here. While the chip is red, the engine is yours, the hull is mine.

**Player options:** How many runs to zero? / Arcadis promised to clear this. / Just let me launch.

**Mara:** Arcadis promised many people. He left you work. First job is on the board: beacon Grid-12 is silent. Bring the log — first debt layer comes off.

Teach: launch, flight, scan, return. The log contains a Thread map fragment and Arcadis’s note: “Do not give this to Vector.”

### Act I — Debt and other people’s smiles (Wedge systems)

1. **Silence on the Grid** — recon, minor pirates, log. Nex warns two parties already asked about the recording.
2. **Caravan to Anchor** — escort; fight / pay-off / abandon (reputation vs money).
3. **Tarek’s man** — sell fragment / refuse / haggle. Consequences on fuel access and later heat.
4. **Grey box** — sealed cargo heats and signals mid-flight; deliver, open, or hand to Guard.
5. **Cashier node** — first Thread site; drones; map expands; Rinn broadcasts an order to report.
6. **Talk on the beacon** — cooperate, defy, or lie to Ring Guard.
7–9. **Pressure from three sides** — Vector contract, local house defense, Ash “only scan and leave”.
10. **Dock under lien** — attack or debt auction on Dry Dock 9. Resolve Mara’s arc for Act I.

**Act I outcomes:** debt cleared or transformed; path toward outer ring opens; Orion Pathfinders is a known name.

### Act II — Korvax and the price of the map

Gate transition into Korvax. Different beacon UI tint, docking rules, local houses.

Mission spine (12–18 beats): first contact law, Arcadis trail on a ghost station, Thread archive puzzle + combat, liaison betrayal, temporary alliance against a rival power, network warden mini-boss, refugee corridor, reveal that Arcadis tried to **close** the Thread, cult activates a dangerous node, player chooses to stop the ritual / embed their key / hand the key to Rinn or Tarek.

**Arcadis full log:**
> “I thought the map would save the Wedge. It only strengthens whoever holds it. The strong on the frontier are not kind for long. If you reached this place, do not continue my route. Finish it.”

### Act III — Endings

Branches (each 2–3 missions):

- Give network to Vector → order and tariffs; border freedom dies.
- Give to Ring Guard → militarized safe corridors; hard control.
- Destroy key nodes → chaos; no absolute owner.
- Keep control under Orion Pathfinders → player becomes route-keeper under constant pressure.

Epilogue factors: Thread control, Dock 9 fate, reputation, companion alive, company as house vs one ship again.

## 6. Dialogue rules (production)

1. NPC lines: 1–3 sentences.
2. Player usually gets 2–4 replies: practical / pressure / human / refuse.
3. Important choices echo after 10–30 minutes of play (price, access, a line of dialogue).
4. No lore lectures — reveal through jobs, debt, and threat.
5. Subtitle-ready: avoid walls of text.

### Sample — Tarek offer

**Tarek:** Captain. You look like someone counting hours until a red chip.
**Player:** Talk. / If this is Vector, the price must exceed the smile. / I dislike being read aloud.
**Tarek:** Then without poetry. Map fragment. We get exclusivity. You get debt relief and fuel priority in three docks.
**Player:** Take it. / Copy only; original stays. / No.
**Tarek (copy):** A copy is courtesy. The original is the market.
**Tarek (no):** A pity. The market does not take offense. It only raises tariffs.

### Sample — Rhea after first joint fight

**Rhea:** You shoot better than you lie.
**Player:** Compliment? 
**Rhea:** Observation. In the Guard they promoted people like that fast… and wrote them off fast.
**Player:** Why leave? / I need repairs, not your past. / Afraid I’ll sell out?
**Rhea (why):** Because “hold the corridor” sometimes means “leave civilians outside the brackets.” I do not do brackets.
**Rhea (sell out):** I am not afraid of a sale. I am afraid you will buy yourself too cheap.

### Sample — Ilsa on a node frequency

**Ilsa:** Hear the clicks in the transmitter? Not noise. Route memory.
**Player:** I need coordinates, not a sermon.
**Ilsa:** Coordinates are also faith. Vector believes in price. The Ring believes in statute. We believe the network wants to be open.
**Player:** The network is hardware.
**Ilsa:** Hardware that chooses whom to pass. You are already chosen. The question is what you become after.
